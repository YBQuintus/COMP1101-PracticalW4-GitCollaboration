P3: Weather Forecasting and Climate Modeling

Weather forecasting and climate modelling are both fields that have come a long way since the early days of computing, and have been greatly improved by the use of modern computing technology.

These fields rely on complex mathematical models and large amounts of data to make predictions about future weather patterns and long-term climate trends. Using new models dervied from past weather data, predicting weather has become more accurate (out to a few days, at least), and climate models have become more sophisticated, allowing for better understanding of the potential impacts of climate change on the planet, and also predicting future climate scenarios based on different emissions scenarios and other factors.
