P2: Global Messaging and Communication

Advances in computing and technology have allowed for people to communicate and share information across the globe in real time.

Applications like WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, and WeChat have allowed for, say, someone from China to contact someone from the USA, and vice versa, in a matter of seconds, and social media platforms like Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter have allowed for the massive spread of information, whether true or false, across the globe to everyone with access to these platforms. This growth in communication and information sharing has allowed for the rapid spread of knowledge, ideas, and culture across the globe, and is a pretty big part of the general trend of globalisation as a whole!
