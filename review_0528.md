# Peer Review

## Interest of content
The website covers useful applications of computing. The medical imaging page is particularly interesting and includes a helpful example and sources.

## Consistency and quality of visual presentation
The main topic pages have a consistent layout and colour scheme. The text is generally easy to read.

## Quality of written presentation
The writing is understandable. Please correct two small spelling errors on the homepage: “Forcasting” to “Forecasting” and “Instragram” to “Instagram”.

## Validity of HTML
The pages opened successfully in the browser. The About and Contact links on the homepage and 3D rendering page have no matching sections. Removing these unused links would be a simple improvement. A full HTML validation check was not performed.

## Evidence of collaboration
The main topic pages include author details. The pages alone do not confirm whether these details were written by another member or whether contributions were balanced.
